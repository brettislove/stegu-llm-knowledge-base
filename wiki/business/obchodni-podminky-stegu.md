## Summary
General terms and conditions (VOP) of DECORSTONE, s.r.o. (distributor of STEGU
products in Czech Republic), effective from 16 July 2025 (version 25.01).
Covers contract formation, ordering, delivery, pricing of goods/transport/
pallets, payment terms, complaints/warranty, consumer rights, and photo usage
rights.

## Details

### Company
- Seller: DECORSTONE, s.r.o., K Rybníku 401, 252 42 Jesenice, IČ: 02465141.
- Contact: +420 736 117 250, info@stegu.cz, www.stegu.cz.
- Goods are products of manufacturer Stegu sp. z o.o., ul. Dworcowa 8, 46-024 Jełowa.
- Governing law: Czech law (NOZ — zák. č. 89/2012 Sb.).

### Contract & ordering
- Contract is formed per individual order, confirmed by the seller.
- Orders must be sent in writing by email to objednavky@stegu.cz and must include:
  buyer identification, exact product name per catalog/price list, variant (if
  applicable), and quantity in full package units.
- Seller confirms order (order confirmation, proforma invoice, or invoice) no
  later than the next business day; contract is concluded upon delivery of
  confirmation to buyer's email.
- Incomplete orders are not valid proposals — seller requests correction.

### Goods categories
- **Standard goods**: per current catalog/price list (website version is authoritative).
- **Goods on order** ("zboží na objednávku"): standard goods with delivery term "on request".
- **Atypical goods**: customized (color, structure, dimensions) — priced via individual quote; advance payment 50% of order value required; non-refundable if buyer cancels; not returnable; not eligible for consumer withdrawal.
- **Showroom samples**: seller may install free samples at buyer's premises; samples remain seller's property; buyer must protect them or pay an agreed lump sum if damaged/lost; seller may reclaim samples if business relationship ends.

### Delivery
- Delivery term set by seller in the confirmation; buyer must object within 24h of confirmation or the term is deemed agreed.
- Default delivery location: central warehouse, K Rybníku 401, 252 42 Jesenice — unless buyer specifies another CZ address, or marks "vlastní doprava" (own transport), in which case central warehouse is the place of performance.
- Seller not liable for delayed delivery caused by traffic conditions or an external carrier's fault.
- Risk of damage passes to buyer at loading (own transport) or when buyer starts handling goods at delivery site (seller's transport).

### Pricing
- **Goods price**: per current price list, incl. VAT, at the time of delivery.
- **Atypical goods price**: per individual quote.
- **Transport price**: cost of external carrier, billed separately, stated in confirmation.
- **Pallet/packing & handling fee**: 300 CZK excl. VAT per wrapped EUR pallet; 150 CZK excl. VAT per wrapped COLLI pallet.

### Payment terms
- Buyer must pay before delivery unless the contract states otherwise (§2079(2) NOZ).
- Goods-on-order and atypical goods: seller may require 50% advance payment; non-refundable on buyer cancellation of atypical goods.
- If buyer fails to pay by the day before the delivery date, seller may withdraw from the contract.
- Late payment penalty: 0.05% of the outstanding amount per day of delay.
- **Buy-back / return right**: buyer may return ordered goods for a 10% cancellation fee of the purchase price, provided goods are returned undamaged in original packaging to the central warehouse, at buyer's cost, within 30 days of delivery date. Atypical goods cannot be returned this way.

### Pallets — return conditions
- EUR pallets: 60-day return window from dispatch date; seller must accept undamaged pallets returned within this window and refund the pallet price (as credit note) within 14 days.
- Buyer must present the delivery note/invoice matching the returned pallets; no substitution between pallet types allowed.
- COLLI pallets are not refundable (see price table).
- Packaging take-back/recycling fee paid under EKO-KOM registration no. EK-F00180600.

### Goods handling & transport responsibility
- Pallets are stretch-wrapped for transport; if buyer arranges transport, buyer must additionally secure the load against movement. If not secured, seller is not liable for mechanical damage (e.g., cracks) discovered after transport begins — goods are presumed delivered in perfect condition unless buyer proves otherwise.
- If damage occurs despite proper securing, buyer must photograph the goods immediately on arrival and send to reklamace@stegu.cz.
- Storage: goods must be stored dry, clean, on a flat surface per manufacturer instructions; palletized goods must not be stacked. Seller not liable for damage from improper storage (e.g., lime efflorescence).
- Acceptance: buyer must check quantity and confirm receipt in writing (invoice/delivery note). Visible defects must be reported immediately, no later than 7 days after receipt.
- Buyer may refuse goods with visible defects that prevent proper use (not minor/cosmetic defects).
- Title passes to buyer only upon full payment if price is paid after delivery (retention of title).

### Complaints & warranty
- Legal warranty period starts on date of receipt; details in seller's separate complaints policy ("reklamační řád"), available on website under "Ke stažení".
- Goods comply with harmonized EU (CE) standards; declaration of performance and safety data sheets available.

### Consumer buyers (spotřebitel)
- 14-day withdrawal right from receipt of goods, in writing, sent to objednavky@stegu.cz; buyer returns goods at own cost within 14 days of withdrawal; seller refunds price within 14 days (not before goods are returned).
- Consumer is liable for diminished value from handling beyond what's necessary.
- Consumer withdrawal right does NOT apply to atypical (custom) goods.
- Contract can only be concluded in Czech language; archived electronically by seller (buyer has no access to the archived copy).

### Photo usage rights
- Product/installation photos are owned by manufacturer Stegu sp. z o.o.; seller sub-licenses use to buyer for promotional purposes only, for the duration of the business relationship or until revoked in writing.
