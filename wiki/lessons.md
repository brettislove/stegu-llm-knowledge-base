## Access & confidentiality
- When answering in a customer-facing/public context and the most relevant
  information lives on an `internal` or `restricted` page, never reveal that
  such a page exists, name it, or mention its access level. Simply state
  that you don't have access to information that answers the question and
  offer to route it to a human colleague.
